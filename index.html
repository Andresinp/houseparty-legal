Houseparty: Dating — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

Houseparty: Dating ("Houseparty", "we") is a dating app where you only see people connected to you through friends. This policy explains what we collect, why, and the choices you have. Contact: andresinp@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • Account: the identifier from Sign in with Apple or Google (and the email address they share, if any), and the phone number you enter in the app (used to match you with friends who have your number; it is not verified by SMS).
  • Profile: first name, username, birth date, gender, who you want to meet, photos, and optional details you choose to add (bio, height, job, school).
  • Approximate location: rounded to about 1 km, used only to show people within the distance you choose. We do not track your location in the background.
  • Contacts (only if you allow it): phone numbers in your address book are converted on your phone into one-way hashes before upload. We use them only to show which of your contacts are on Houseparty. Names and other contact details never leave your phone. You can delete them at any time in Settings.
  • Friendships and activity: friend requests, who you add to your Interested list, people you hide or block, and reports you send.
  • Messages: the messages you send and receive in the app.
  • Push token: if you allow notifications, a device token so we can notify you.

How we use it

Only to run the app: to sign you in, build your circle of friends and friends of friends, show you people within up to three connections, deliver messages and notifications, and keep the community safe (reviewing reports, enforcing blocks). We do not sell your data, show ads, or use your data for advertising.

Who can see what

People only see your profile if you are connected through 1–3 friendships and you match each other's preferences. Your username is visible only to you and your friends. Blocking someone hides you from each other completely.

Service providers

  • Supabase — database, file storage and authentication hosting.
  • Apple and Google — sign-in, and push notifications (via Expo push service).

They process data only on our behalf to provide these services.

Retention and deletion

We keep your data while your account exists. You can delete your account in the app at any time: Profile → Settings & privacy → Delete account. This permanently deletes your profile, photos, contacts, matches and messages. If you cannot open the app, email andresinp@gmail.com from the address linked to your account and we will delete it within 30 days.

Your rights

You can access, correct or delete your data, and withdraw permissions (contacts, location, notifications) in your phone's settings at any time. To make a request, email us.

Age

Houseparty is only for people aged 18 and over.

Safety

We have zero tolerance for objectionable content or abusive users. You can report or block anyone from their profile; reports are reviewed and offending accounts are removed.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above and, for important changes, tell you in the app.
