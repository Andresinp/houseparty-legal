How Houseparty works

You only see people connected to you through friends — up to three connections away. Add your friends by username or from your contacts, and your circle grows through theirs.

Report or block someone

Open their profile and tap Report or Block. Blocking hides you from each other completely. Reports are reviewed and abusive accounts are removed.

Delete your account

In the app: Profile → Settings & privacy → Delete account. This permanently deletes your profile, photos, contacts and messages. If you can't open the app, email us from the address linked to your account and we'll delete it within 30 days.
